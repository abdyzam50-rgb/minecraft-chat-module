## Money, by progression stage

Early game (no gear, under ~10m):
- The Forge — refined mithril on a 6h cycle, roughly 3-4m/day for very little effort
- NPC flipping — buy enchanted diamond blocks on bazaar order, sell to NPC
- Experimentation table, once enchanting is up
- The Rift — cheap to max and there are several income routes inside it
- Zealots in the End — summoning eyes to bazaar; wants zealuck and an enderman pet

NOT early game, do not suggest these to a new player:
- f7 / master mode dungeons — endgame, needs a full setup and a party
- Slayer t4/t5 — needs real gear first
- Ghost grinding (what 3172 does) — needs magic find, a decent setup and patience

Mid game: dungeons from f5 up, slayer t3-t4, gemstone mining, forge flipping.

Late game: f7/m7 carries, ghosts, high-tier slayers, large-scale AH flipping.

## This grind

- Ghosts live in The Mist, in the Dwarven Mines. Lv250, they walk at you and hit
  for ~1000 plus true damage per second inside 4 blocks.
- Drops: Sorrow, Volta, Plasma, Ghostly Boots. Sorrow is the rare one (~0.12%),
  affected by magic find and looting.
- Magic find and bestiary matter more than raw damage.
- It is the most macro-accused grind in the game, which is why the checks happen.
